Exercise 1 · Stationery shop · about 30 min

Write the program of the till of a stationery shop.

  • Data: the shop is called Paper & Co, its owner is Vicent, a pen costs 1.25 euros, the VAT is 21 % and a box has 12 pens.
  • Input: ask the customer's name, how many pens they want and how much money they give (it can have decimals, for example 40.5).

Show in the page, each thing on its own line:

  1. A greeting with the name of the customer and the name of the shop.
  2. How many complete boxes and how many loose pens they take.
  3. The price without VAT, the VAT and the total.
  4. The change, and the change split into whole euros and cents.
  5. true or false for each of these sentences:
    • The customer gave enough money.
    • They get a free bag: they take 24 pens or more, or the total is more than 30 euros.
    • The customer is the owner (the name is exactly the name of the owner).
    • They get a discount next time: they are not the owner and they take at least one complete box.
  6. The type of the three values you asked (typeof). They must be a text and two numbers.

Also, write the three values you asked in the console, and write a comment next to the conversion that explains why you need it and what would happen without it.

Example: Ana, 20 pens, she gives 40 Hello, Ana! Welcome to Paper & Co.
20 pens: 1 boxes and 8 loose pens
Price: 25.00 euros · VAT (21 %): 5.25 euros · Total: 30.25 euros
Change: 9.75 euros = 9 euros and 75 cents
Enough money: true · Free bag: true · The customer is the owner: false · Discount next time: true
Types: string, number, number

Tip: Math.floor(x) takes the whole part of a number and Math.round(x) rounds it to the nearest whole number.

Exercise 2 · The film · about 15 min

Ask how many minutes a film lasts, and the hour and the minute when it starts (two questions). Show:

  1. How long it lasts in hours and minutes.
  2. At what time it ends. If it ends after midnight, the hour starts again from 0.
  3. true or false: it is a long film (more than 120 minutes).
  4. true or false: it ends after midnight.
Example: 135 minutes, starts at 22:50 It lasts 2 h 15 min
It ends at 1:05
Long film: true
It ends after midnight: true

Tip: count everything in minutes from 0:00. A day has 24 × 60 minutes. If you write 1:5 instead of 1:05 it is fine. Extra: String(5).padStart(2, "0") gives "05".

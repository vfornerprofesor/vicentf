Exercici 3.1

Exercise 3.1. Build a page about your favourite video game, with an h1 (the name of the game), three h2 sections (Story, How to play, Why I like it) and two paragraphs under each one.

Solució

game.html 
<!DOCTYPE html>
<html lang="en">

<head>
    <meta charset="utf-8">
    <title>Minecraft</title>
</head>

<body>
    <h1>Minecraft</h1>

    <h2>Story</h2>
    <p>You arrive in a world made of blocks, with no instructions and no map.</p>
    <p>Nobody tells you what to do: the story is the one you build.</p>

    <h2>How to play</h2>
    <p>You cut trees, you dig and you craft tools to survive the first night.</p>
    <p>You can play alone or with your friends in the same world.</p>

    <h2>Why I like it</h2>
    <p>I like it because there are no rules about what you must build.</p>
    <p>Every game is different, so I never get bored.</p>
</body>

</html>

Resultat

        Spain
    </p>
</body>

</html>

Exercici 3.3

Exercise 3.3. Create a page with: a title (h1) with your name, a horizontal line (hr), a paragraph about your favourite music band, an image of the band, a paragraph about your favourite film and an image of the film. Remember the <img> tag from lesson 2: <img src="..." alt="...">. Save the images in the same folder as the page.

Solució

index.html 
<!DOCTYPE html>
<html lang="en">

<head>
    <meta charset="utf-8">
    <title>Marc Ferrer</title>
</head>

<body>
    <h1>Marc Ferrer</h1>
    <hr>
    <p>My favourite band is Muse. I like their guitars and the way they sound live.</p>
    <img src="band.jpg" alt="Muse playing on a stage with blue lights" width="400">
    <p>My favourite film is Interstellar. I have seen it four times.</p>
    <img src="film.jpg" alt="Poster of Interstellar with an astronaut in front of a planet" width="400">
</body>

</html>
Les imatges band.jpg i film.jpg han d'estar en la mateixa carpeta que la pàgina. En la previsualització es mostren com a marcador.

Resultat

Exercici 3.4

Exercise 3.4. Write a paragraph that shows this text in the browser, exactly: The tag <h1> & the tag <p> are different.

Solució

entities.html 
<!DOCTYPE html>
<html lang="en">

<head>
    <meta charset="utf-8">
    <title>Entities</title>
</head>

<body>
    <p>The tag &lt;h1&gt; &amp; the tag &lt;p&gt; are different.</p>
</body>

</html>
Els símbols < i & s'escriuen com a entitats (&lt; i &amp;) perquè el navegador no els confonga amb una etiqueta.

Resultat

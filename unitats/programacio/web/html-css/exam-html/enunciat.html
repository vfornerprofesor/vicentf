HTML exam · The Repair Café

Name: ______________________________ Group: ________ Date: ____/____/________ Time: 55 minutes

The SMX students organise a Repair Café: one Saturday they repair the old computers and phones of the town. You make its small website: two pages written from zero.

Before you start

  • Create a folder called surname-name-html and copy inside it the images folder from the starter folder (it has pc.svg).
  • Inside your folder create event.html (exercise 1) and join.html (exercise 2). Write all the code yourself: do not copy it from any other file.
  • You can use VS Code, the browser and the W3C validator. You cannot use notes, the lessons, the internet to search for answers, a mobile phone or any AI tool.
  • Save often. At the end, hand in the whole folder.

How your code is marked

  • Each page has the complete skeleton: doctype, language English, UTF-8 and its own title.
  • At the top of the body, a comment with your name and your group.
  • Every tag closed and well nested, and the code indented. The headings go in order (h1, then h2…).
  • Use the semantic tag that fits each part. Do not use div.
  • The validator must say 0 errors for each page.

Exercise 1 · The event page (event.html) · about 35 min

The title of the page is SMX Repair Café. The page has three parts.

  1. Top. The heading SMX Repair Café and a menu with two links: Home (to event.html) and Join us (to join.html).
  2. Main content, with four parts. Each one has a heading.
    • About the event: a paragraph that says the event is on Saturday 14 November (in bold, because it is important) and that it is free (stressed) & open to everybody. Write the & the right way. Under the text, the picture images/pc.svg with a description for blind users and the caption Our repair desk in the computer lab.
    • What we fix: the list of the picture below.
    • Timetable: the table of the picture below. The first row is the header row, the times are headers of their row and Lunch break is one single cell that takes all the columns.
    • Last year: a short news item that could be published alone in another website: We repaired 42 devices in one day. and a link Book your place to join.html.
  3. Bottom. © 2026 SMX students (write the © the right way), a link that opens an email to repaircafe@example.com, and a link to https://www.w3.org that opens in a new tab.

Exercise 2 · The booking page (join.html) · about 15 min

The title of the page is Join the Repair Café. It has the same top and bottom as event.html (you can copy them from your own page; the link to w3.org is not needed). The main content is the heading Book your place and a form with these fields:

Group Label text What the user writes or chooses Compulsory?
About youNameA short textYes
EmailAn email addressYes
Your deviceDeviceOne option from a list: Laptop, Desktop PC or PhoneYes
What is the problem?A long text, on several linesYes
Do you bring the charger?Yes or No (only one)Yes
Preferred dayA dateNo
(no group)I accept that the repair is not guaranteedA box to tickYes
  • The fields of About you and Your device go in two groups, each with its title.
  • Every field has its label linked to it, the right type for its data and the validation that the table asks for.
  • At the end of the form, a button that sends it with the text Send.

Before you hand in

  • Your folder has event.html, join.html and the images folder.
  • Click every link of both pages: the menu, Book your place, the email and w3.org. The picture must show.
  • Send the form empty: the browser must stop you. Run the validator on both pages.

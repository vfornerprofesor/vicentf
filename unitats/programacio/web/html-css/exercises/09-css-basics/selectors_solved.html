Our school builds its own robot

12 November

The students of 4th ESO have built a robot that follows a black line on the floor. It took them three weeks, two broken motors and a lot of patience.

Next month they will take it to a regional competition. Wish them luck!

How strong is your password?

5 November

A good password is long, has numbers, capital letters and symbols, and is not your pet's name.

Never use the same password for everything.

Tip: a password manager remembers them for you.
